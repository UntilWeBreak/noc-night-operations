Privacy Policy

NOC: Night Operations

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use NOC: Night Operations ("the App").

1. General

NOC: Night Operations is an Android mobile game. The App does not require users to create an account and does not operate its own online user account system.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information from or about the user's device in order to provide, measure and improve advertising. Depending on the user's location and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Information processed may include, depending on applicable settings and consent:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • Diagnostic information
  • Consent and privacy choices

3. Consent Management

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy and advertising consent.

Users in applicable regions may be presented with options to consent, not consent, or manage their privacy choices.

Where required, users can access privacy options within the App to review or change their privacy choices.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about how Google uses information from apps that use Google services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the device.

This may include:

  • Tutorial completion
  • Unlocked game content
  • Shift results and ratings
  • Audio settings
  • Other game preferences

The developer does not operate an account system for synchronizing this game progress with a developer-controlled server.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server system.

7. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services integrated into the App, particularly Google AdMob and Google's consent management services, as described above.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored game data generally remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, cleared by the App, or the App is uninstalled, depending on Android system behavior.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention policies and privacy terms.

9. User Choices

Users may review or change applicable advertising privacy choices through the privacy options provided within the App.

Users can also remove locally stored App data using Android's application settings or by uninstalling the App.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to keep the App and its integrations secure. However, no electronic processing or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App, its features, advertising implementation or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available at this location.
